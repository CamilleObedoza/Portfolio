Overview

This case study examined counterfeit cosmetics as a strategic and operational risk within Amazon's third-party marketplace. The analysis focused specifically on bullet and liquid lip products because their high demand, small size, and direct skin contact make counterfeit products a concern for both customer safety and marketplace trust.

Using SWOT analysis, critical success factors, business issues, performance indicators, financial analysis, and internal and external assessments, we evaluated Amazon's existing capabilities and identified opportunities to strengthen counterfeit prevention.

The analysis resulted in a proposed strategy centered on stronger seller verification, AI-driven counterfeit detection, expanded product authentication, and closer partnerships with verified beauty brands. Based on our financial scenario analysis, reducing the estimated counterfeit-related inefficiencies by 20% could represent approximately $986 million in annual cost savings.
